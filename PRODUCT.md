# EquaLens Product Context

## Product

- **What it is:** A Chrome extension and Cloudflare-backed analysis service that reveals gendered assumptions in web interfaces, explains their impact with evidence, and proposes capability-preserving redesigns.
- **Primary users:** Designers, product teams, accessibility and inclusion reviewers, and hackathon judges evaluating the live demonstration.
- **Core job:** Turn hidden gender defaults into concrete, inspectable findings without replacing one stereotype with another.
- **Platform:** Chrome Manifest V3 extension, Cloudflare Worker API, and a static automotive demonstration site.
- **Success:** A user can select or scan interface content, understand who may be excluded and why, redesign it without losing functionality or information, and export a shareable report.

## Brand

- **Personality:** Precise, evidence-led, calm, humane, and technically credible.
- **Visual language:** EquaLens uses deep teal and cool neutral surfaces; the Meridian Motors demo uses the supplied Obsidian Precision system with sharp geometry, restrained steel-blue accents, and cinematic local imagery.
- **Voice:** Direct and explanatory. Avoid moralizing, caricature, or claims that outrun the evidence.
- **Anti-references:** Generic dashboard templates, playful cartoon styling, exaggerated strawman bias, opaque AI claims, and redesigns that simplify a product “for women.”

## Principles

1. Preserve or increase user capability, information, controls, and choice.
2. Label evidence separately from model inference.
3. Keep user data out of storage except explicitly exported finding reports.
4. Meet WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion preferences, and responsive layouts.
5. Keep the scripted Meridian Motors examples credible enough to resemble a real premium vehicle product page.
